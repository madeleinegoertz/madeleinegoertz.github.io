I am a first-year Ph.D. student in the [mathematics department](https://math.berkeley.edu/) at [UC Berkeley](https://www.berkeley.edu/). In 2025, I graduated from the [blended BS/MS mathematics degree program](https://math.calpoly.edu/degree-blended) at [Cal Poly San Luis Obispo](https://www.calpoly.edu/), where I was a [Frost Research Scholar](https://cosam.calpoly.edu/prospective-students/frost-fund/frost-scholarship) in the [Bailey College of Science and Mathematics](https://cosam.calpoly.edu/). My current research interests are in nonlinear algebra, in particular metric algebraic geometry.
